Reverse logistics & returns

Returns programs built to recover value, not just process volume.

We design, implement, and scale reverse logistics, trade-in, and returns programs that reduce operating costs, preserve asset value, accelerate recovery, and connect products with the highest-value viable recovery channels.

Our approach

From a clear diagnosis to a program that delivers results.

01 — ASSESS

Operational assessment

We start by mapping how products actually come back—across categories, conditions, and channels. This identifies cost drivers, recovery opportunities, and the highest-value path for each asset.

  • Current-state flow and cost mapping
  • Dwell-time and value-loss analysis
  • Asset condition, disposition, and market options
  • A short, prioritized action plan
02 — DESIGN

Program Design

Next, we turn insight into a practical, scalable program—defining the operating model, processes, technology, and commercial channels to maximize recovery and performance.

  • Tailored operating model and process design
  • Clear program standards, workflows, and controls
  • Recovery strategy that maximizes value across all asset outcomes
03 — BUILD

Workflow Design & Process Improvement

We turn program designs into efficient, scalable operations. From receiving and processing to disposition and fulfillment, we streamline workflows, eliminate unnecessary handling, and establish repeatable processes that improve throughput, accuracy, and cost efficiency.

  • Operational workflows for intake, assessment, processing, and disposition
  • Process optimization to reduce handling costs, improve throughput, and minimize errors
  • Standardized procedures and performance controls that support consistent execution across teams and partners
04 — OPERATE & SCALE

Logistics, Processing & Operational Scale

We bring program designs into operation through integrated logistics, warehousing, processing, and inventory management. Intelegistics serves as your operational partner, coordinating the flow of products from receipt through recovery and disposition, while integrating specialized technology and downstream service providers where needed.

Our focus is consistent execution, operational visibility, and scalable performance as volumes grow.

  • 3PL operations, warehousing, inventory management, and processing
  • Technology integration and specialized service-provider coordination
  • Program launch, performance management, and volume scaling
